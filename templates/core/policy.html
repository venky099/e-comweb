Delivery charges

{% elif policy_slug == "returns" %}

Return window

Most items can be returned within {{ RETURN_WINDOW_DAYS }} days of delivery. Products marked non-returnable on the product page are excluded; this is shown before you buy.

Condition

Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and with all tags and accessories included. We may refuse a return that arrives outside this condition.

How to return

  1. Open the order in My orders.
  2. Choose Return this item next to the item, and pick a reason.
  3. We review the request and arrange a pickup.
  4. Your refund is issued once the item reaches our warehouse.

Refunds

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of the item being received. Delivery charges are refunded only when the item was faulty or incorrectly sent. For cash-on-delivery orders we collect your bank details for the refund.

Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled free of charge within {{ ORDER_CANCEL_WINDOW_HOURS }} hours while they remain unshipped. Any payment already taken is refunded in full.

{% elif policy_slug == "privacy" %}

What we collect

We collect your name, email address, phone number and delivery addresses to fulfil your orders, plus your order and browsing history to run the store.

Payment data

We never see or store your card number, CVV or UPI PIN. Payments are processed by our payment gateway; we retain only a transaction reference and the payment status.

How we use it

  • Processing, delivering and supporting your orders.
  • Sending transactional emails such as order confirmations.
  • Marketing emails, only if you opted in — you can turn these off in your profile.

Your rights

You can view and edit your personal details in profile settings, and request deletion of your account by emailing {{ SUPPORT_EMAIL }}. Order records are kept as required for tax and accounting purposes.

Cookies

We use cookies for session management (keeping you signed in and keeping your cart) and for CSRF protection. These are required for the site to function.
